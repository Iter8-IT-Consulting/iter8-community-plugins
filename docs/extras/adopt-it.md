# Adopt It

*Coming soon.*

**Bring an app you've already started onto the journey.**

Not everyone starts from scratch. Maybe you built something before you
found iter8-it, with its own GitHub, Vercel and Supabase. Adopt It works
out where that app is in the journey, fills in what's missing, and lets
every step carry on from there.

## What it will do

1. **Look before touching anything.** It reads your code, your GitHub
   board and Issues, your Vercel and Supabase setup, and how changes reach
   the live site today. Nothing changes yet.
2. **Place it on the journey.** A live app with a backlog is past Trim It,
   for example. It picks up from the right step.
3. **Write down the product story.** It drafts your problem, your people,
   your first version and your layout from what's already there (your
   README, your code, your live site), then checks each with you. It
   won't re-interview you from scratch.
4. **Show the gaps, in plain words.** For each difference from the
   iter8-it way: what it would take to change, the risk, and whether it's
   needed or optional. Sometimes it even saves money, like switching off
   a paid database you don't need.
5. **Close them one at a time, with your OK.** Lowest risk first, each as
   a change you can review, and never anything that could break your
   live app without a way back. Gaps you'd rather keep are written down,
   so the other steps work around them.

Then [What's Next](whats-next.md) takes it from there.
