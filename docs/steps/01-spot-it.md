# Spot It

**Find a real problem in your life or work that a simple app could fix.**

Most app ideas start as a solution: "an app that does X". Spot It goes
one step back, to the problem underneath. Who has it? What do they do
today? What goes wrong? How would you know it's fixed? Get those right
and every later decision gets easier, because you can always ask: does
this help with *that*?

## What happens

A short conversation, one question at a time. Claude asks about the
people with the problem, what they do now, and a recent time it went
wrong. If you start with a solution, it gently asks what that solution
would change. It will also tell you honestly if a spreadsheet or an
existing app would do the job. (Wanting to build it anyway, to learn, is
a fine reason.)

Then it reads the problem back to you in a few plain sentences, and you
adjust them until they're right.

## What you end up with

- A clear problem statement, in your words, in your project's
  `PRODUCT.md`.
- A project folder, ready for the next step.
- Nothing online yet. You can stop here with nothing to undo.

## What it asks of you

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time** | 10-20 minutes |
| **Decisions** | Whether the problem, as written, is the one you want to solve |
| **Cost** | Nothing |

**Bring a partner.** It works with two people at the keyboard. They'll
often remember different parts of the problem.
