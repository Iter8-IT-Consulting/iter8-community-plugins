# Grow It

*Coming soon.*

**Keep improving your app with what you learn from real use.**

Once people are using your app, they'll teach you things no brainstorm
could: what they love, what they ignore, what they wish it did. Grow It
turns that into the next round of work.

## What it will do

- **Gather what you've learned**: feedback, bug patterns, what's used
  and what isn't.
- **Look again at the basics**: are your personas still right? Is the
  problem still the problem?
- **Dream and trim, again**: new ideas go into the Backlog, parked ideas
  get a second look, and the board is reordered for the next release.
- **Suggest upgrades when they'd pay off**, each with its cost stated:
  your own domain name, error alerts, usage numbers, branded emails, a
  staging copy of your app for testing, and more. Never assumed, always
  offered.

Until Grow It arrives, [Dream It](05-dream-it.md) and
[Trim It](06-trim-it.md) cover most of this: run them whenever you've
learned something.
