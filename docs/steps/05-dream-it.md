# Dream It

**Get every feature idea out of your head and onto the table, big or
small.**

This is the brainstorm. No judging, no "that's too hard", no "we'll never
need that". Every idea is welcome, because the best ones often sit right
next to the silly ones. Choosing comes later, in Trim It.

## What happens

Claude brainstorms with you, one persona at a time: "What would make the
Presenter's day easier?" It adds suggestions of its own, clearly marked,
and keeps going until you run dry. Then it shapes the pile:

| | What it is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Epic** | A big area of the app | *Joining a talk* |
| **Feature** | Something a persona can do | *Audience Member can join a talk by scanning a QR code* |

You review the grouped list and change anything you like. Only then does
it go on the board.

## What you end up with

- Every idea as a card on your board, in the **Backlog** column, grouped
  under its Epic and tagged with the persona it's for.
- One place to see everything you might build.

Nothing is planned yet. Backlog means "ideas", not "to-do".

## What it asks of you

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time** | 20-40 minutes. The best part. |
| **Decisions** | None, on purpose. Just ideas. |
| **Cost** | Nothing |

**Run it again any time.** New idea in the shower? Dream It adds it to the
Backlog without disturbing anything else.
